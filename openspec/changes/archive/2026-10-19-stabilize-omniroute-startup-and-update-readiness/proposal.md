# Proposal

## Why

The local OmniRoute gateway container (`diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest`, `v3.8.51`) was stuck in a ~6–8 minute startup and watchdog restart loop because synchronous SQLite startup health checks (`runDbHealthCheck` + `scanQuotaSnapshots`) on the 4.03 GB (`3.75 GiB`) `/app/data/storage.sqlite` database blocked the single-threaded Node.js event loop for ~270–300 seconds, exceeding Docker's healthcheck window (`start_period: 120s` + 3 retries = ~210–255s) and causing `com.omniroute.watchdog` to issue `SIGTERM` right as initialization completed.

In addition, `/app/data` on the `omniroute-data` Compose volume (Docker Engine volume `omniroute_omniroute-data`) held 9.07 GB across `storage.sqlite` (4.03 GB with 4,859,612 `conversation_turn_nodes` rows from `O(N^2)` divergence duplication and 425,838 `quota_snapshots` rows), an orphaned `storage.sqlite.snapshot.bak` (3.02 GB from 2026-10-10), and `/app/data/db_backups/` (2.02 GB from 2026-10-03), while local `~/Omniroute` (`fd95f823d`) was 3 commits behind `origin/main` (`e62b4b71c`) and upstream is actively stabilizing `release/v3.8.52` (`7d7ed3f3a`, adding 15 SQLite migrations `197`–`211` including `conversation_turn_nodes` index rebuilds in `201` and `211` plus pre-migration `VACUUM INTO` backups). Post-apply audit further identified that PID/epoch text lockfiles (`~/.omniroute/update.lock`) are non-atomic under concurrent acquisition, unsafe when wall-clock age expiry (`1800s`) or malformed metadata reaps a live lock owner, and fragile when unlinked across contenders; that `OMNIROUTE_SKIP_DB_HEALTHCHECK=1` skips `PRAGMA quick_check` globally across all managed health checks (`core.ts:954`) so an out-of-band periodic read-only full `PRAGMA integrity_check` is needed; and that the 2026-10-19 cleanup verified a 2026-10-03 (20-provider) snapshot rather than a fresh 32-provider backup.

## What Changes

- **Non-Blocking Startup Deferral, Managed Worker Contention Protection & Out-of-Band Integrity Policy (`~/Omniroute/docker-compose.override.yml` & `omniroute-watchdog.sh`)**:
  - Retain `DB_HEALTHCHECK_STARTUP_DEFERRED_ENABLED=true` (deferring startup DB health verification out of `getDbInstance()` via `setImmediate` into `healthCheckWorker.ts` per `#13717`) and `OMNIROUTE_SKIP_DB_HEALTHCHECK=1` on `omniroute-base`. In `src/lib/db/core.ts:954`, `OMNIROUTE_SKIP_DB_HEALTHCHECK=1` globally skips `PRAGMA quick_check` across deferred startup, 6-hour periodic, and manual `POST /api/db/health` checks—preventing `healthCheckWorker.ts` from re-running a ~6.45s `PRAGMA quick_check` + 321k-row `scanQuotaSnapshots` scan inside `connection.immediate()` (`BEGIN IMMEDIATE`) when `autoRepair` triggers.
  - Because `OMNIROUTE_SKIP_DB_HEALTHCHECK=1` suppresses in-app physical B-tree checks, add a Step 3 periodic read-only full `PRAGMA integrity_check` to `~/Developer/scripts/omniroute-watchdog.sh` (every 6 hours / `21600s` gated by `~/.omniroute/integrity-last-ok` mtime when the container is `running`, `health != starting`, and `/healthz` returns HTTP 200), bounded by an in-container child timeout of `110s` (`SIGKILL`) and outer `gtimeout --signal=TERM --kill-after=5s 120s`, touching `~/.omniroute/integrity-last-ok` only when all rows equal `'ok'` and logging/exiting non-zero on failure **without** restarting an otherwise healthy gateway.
  - Preserve `NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=4096`, loopback-only port bindings (`127.0.0.1:20128-20129`, `127.0.0.1:20132`), named volume `omniroute-data:/app/data` (`omniroute_omniroute-data`), and `entrypoint: ["/app/container-entrypoint-compat.sh"]`.
- **SQLite Storage Reclamation, Retention Hygiene & Post-Compaction Host Backup (`omniroute-data` / `omniroute_omniroute-data` `/app/data`)**:
  - Remove the orphaned 3.02 GB `/app/data/storage.sqlite.snapshot.bak` and superseded 2.02 GB pre-migration backups in `/app/data/db_backups/`, upsert both flat legacy keys and canonical dotted `retention.*` keys in `key_value` (`namespace = 'databaseSettings'`: `conversationTurnNodes` / `retention.conversationTurnNodes = '7'`, `quotaSnapshots` / `retention.quotaSnapshots = '14'`, `callLogs` / `retention.callLogs = '30'`), prune expired `conversation_turn_nodes` (7d ISO-8601 cutoff), orphaned `agentic_conversations` (7d ISO-8601 cutoff), and `quota_snapshots` (14d ISO-8601 cutoff) using a `1000:1000` (`node:node`) one-shot container, and run offline `PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)` + `VACUUM` so `storage.sqlite` shrinks from ~4.03 GB to ~1.07–1.11 GB (`PRAGMA auto_vacuum = 2` / `INCREMENTAL`, `scheduler/vacuumDrift = "null"`).
  - Require offline maintenance pruning and `databaseSettings` upserts to execute in a joint transaction with fail-closed postcondition assertions (`PRAGMA quick_check = ok` and preserved `api_keys`, `provider_connections`, and `combos` counts), and capture a fresh post-compaction host snapshot in `~/.omniroute/snapshots/` (`api_keys = 1`, `provider_connections = 32`, `combos = 5`) under `/usr/bin/lockf -k -t 0 ~/.omniroute/update.lock` to close the recovery gap left by the historical 2026-10-03 (20-provider) snapshot.
- **Kernel Descriptor Lock Coordination (`/usr/bin/lockf`), Provenance Mirroring & `update.sh` Hardening**:
  - Replace PID/epoch/age/unlink lockfile handling in `~/Developer/scripts/omniroute-watchdog.sh` (mirrored byte-for-byte to `/Users/androidteam/Developer/platform/openspec-store/scripts/omniroute-watchdog.sh` per `config/script-provenance-manifest.tsv`) and `~/Omniroute/scripts/update.sh` with macOS native `/usr/bin/lockf` kernel descriptor locking on persistent inode `~/.omniroute/update.lock` (`umask 077`, `exec 9>>"$LOCK_FILE"`, `/usr/bin/lockf -s -t 0 9`), never writing PID/epoch metadata, expiring live owners by age, or unlinking `~/.omniroute/update.lock` (and closing `9>&-` on child invocations in `omniroute-watchdog.sh`).
  - Serialize the entire `omniroute-watchdog.sh` cycle (Steps 1–3) under FD `9` (exiting `0` on lock contention exit code `75`, failing closed non-zero on other lock errors). In `update.sh`, acquire FD `9` after Gate 0 and argument validation before `pre-state` (retaining `~/.omniroute/update-available` and exiting `1` on contention `75`), clean up in-container `/tmp/${SNAPSHOT_NAME}` in the `EXIT` trap before shell exit releases FD `9` while preserving the original exit status, bound `docker`/`sqlite3` probes and backup with `gtimeout` (`run_bounded`), document `{readonly:true, timeout:15000}` as SQLite `busy_timeout`, switch Gate 1 and Gate 9 to `PRAGMA quick_check`, require Gate 1 host `PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)` to return `0|*` (`busy == 0`) before removing `-wal`/`-shm` sidecars, and re-verify post-checkpoint host `PRAGMA quick_check = ok`.
  - Fast-forward local `~/Omniroute` `main` (`fd95f823d`) to `origin/main` (`e62b4b71c`), harden `~/.local/bin/omniroute` so `status` queries live daemon endpoints (`/api/monitoring/health` + `/api/omniroute/status`, failing non-zero when unreachable) and `keys list` queries canonical `GET /api/providers?limit=5000` (`authType === "apikey" && apiKey`, masked metadata only, failing closed when unreachable instead of falling back to the empty ephemeral bridge SQLite file), verify live runtime health via remote-backed HTTP and CLI checks (`omniroute health --json` and `omniroute quota status`), and add behavioral pytest coverage in `/Users/androidteam/Developer/platform/openspec-store/tests/test_omniroute_lock_and_watchdog.py` and `/Users/androidteam/Developer/platform/openspec-store/tests/test_omniroute_cli_bridge.py`.

## Non-Goals

- Switching `/app/data` from the `omniroute-data` (`omniroute_omniroute-data`) Docker named volume to a host bind mount (prohibited due to macOS VirtioFS SQLite WAL corruption).
- Deploying an untagged `v3.8.52` or `main` image before upstream publishes the official `v3.8.52` release to `diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest` on Docker Hub.
- Modifying tracked upstream TypeScript source files under `~/Omniroute/src/` or `~/Omniroute/open-sse/`.
- Modifying `api_keys`, `provider_connections`, `combos`, or coding-agent CLI routing configurations.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `bifrost-gateway`: Require non-blocking startup database healthcheck deferral (`DB_HEALTHCHECK_STARTUP_DEFERRED_ENABLED=true`) and global managed `quick_check` skip (`OMNIROUTE_SKIP_DB_HEALTHCHECK=1`) paired with out-of-band integrity verification, bounded SQLite storage/snapshot hygiene inside `omniroute-data` (`omniroute_omniroute-data`), and native `/usr/bin/lockf` kernel descriptor lock coordination plus bounded `gtimeout` probes and fail-closed snapshot checkpoint verification in `update.sh` (keeping requirement descriptions `<= 500` characters).
- `docker-lifecycle`: Require `omniroute-watchdog.sh` (and its provenance-tracked mirror in `openspec-store/scripts/omniroute-watchdog.sh`) to serialize its entire cycle via a non-blocking `/usr/bin/lockf` kernel lock on `~/.omniroute/update.lock` (FD `9`) at Step 0 and execute a bounded 6-hour read-only full `PRAGMA integrity_check` (`~/.omniroute/integrity-last-ok`) at Step 3 that logs and exits non-zero on failure without restarting a healthy gateway.

## Impact

- **Availability**: Eliminates the ~270–300s main-thread startup stall, watchdog `SIGTERM` crash loop, and `healthCheckWorker.ts` `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` `quick_check` write-lock stalls, while enforcing 6-hour read-only full `PRAGMA integrity_check` monitoring out of band.
- **Disk & I/O**: Reclaims ~7.54 GiB inside the `omniroute_omniroute-data` Docker volume and secures a fresh post-compaction host snapshot of all 32 live `provider_connections`.
- **Upgrade Readiness for `v3.8.52`**: Pre-shrinks `conversation_turn_nodes` before `v3.8.52` migrations `201` and `211` and guarantees race-free `/usr/bin/lockf` kernel serialization across `com.omniroute.watchdog`, manual maintenance, and `~/Omniroute/scripts/update.sh`.
