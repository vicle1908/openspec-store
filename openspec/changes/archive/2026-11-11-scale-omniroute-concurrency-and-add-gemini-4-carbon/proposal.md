# Proposal

## Why

OmniRoute (`v3.8.53` on `127.0.0.1:20128` / `127.0.0.1:20129`) currently caps immediate heavy-request admission at 16 primary slots with a 4 MB parked-queue byte limit (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_MAX_QUEUED_BYTES`), concentrates burst traffic onto a single Antigravity account (`62af823b`, drained to 4.2% while sibling Ultra accounts sit at 96.1%–96.2%) due to `strategy: "auto"` thundering-herd selection, leaks 429 errors across 7 unentitled Pro accounts in `gemini-4-argon*`, and has not yet exposed Google's newly available `gemini-4-carbon-{high,medium,low}` (`1,048,576` context window, `262,144` max output tokens) combos across the gateway and coding-agent fleet. We need a pure configuration-driven upgrade—with zero modifications to tracked OmniRoute source files—that scales concurrent heavy-request capacity to 64 active streams, eliminates multi-account thundering-herd and quota-bleed bottlenecks via `quota-weighted` routing, and operationalizes `gemini-4-carbon{,-high,-medium,-low}` so `~/Omniroute/scripts/update.sh` can continue pulling official upstream releases cleanly.

## What Changes

- **Scale Heavy Request Admission, In-Flight Byte Budgets, and Libuv Threadpool via Configuration (`~/Omniroute/.env` & `~/Omniroute/docker-compose.override.yml`)**:
  - Increase `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` from `16` to `32` (expanding total non-queued heavy admission capacity with healthy V8 heap headroom from `32` to `64` concurrent requests).
  - Explicitly set `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_MAX_QUEUED_BYTES=67108864` (`64 MB`, up from the 4 MB default that sheds parked requests after ~4× 1 MB agent bodies), `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES=268435456` (`256 MB`, up from the 137.36 MB auto-derived ceiling), `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=30000` (`30s`), and `UV_THREADPOOL_SIZE=32` (up from Node's default 4 threads for parallel zlib decompression and DNS resolution).
  - Propagate all admission and threadpool variables explicitly in `~/Omniroute/docker-compose.override.yml` (`environment:`) while keeping `~/Omniroute` tracked Git working tree completely clean (`git status --porcelain --untracked-files=no` empty) and fast-forwarding local `main` to `origin/main`.
- **Operationalize Google Gemini 4 Carbon (`gemini-4-carbon{,-high,-medium,-low}`) via SQLite Configuration & Capability Overrides**:
  - Register runtime capability and context overrides via 12 single-key `PATCH /api/model-capability-overrides` requests for `agy/gemini-4-carbon-high`, `agy/gemini-4-carbon-medium`, and `agy/gemini-4-carbon-low` (`context_length: 1048576`, `max_input_tokens: 1048576`, `max_output_tokens: 262144`, and comma-separated string `reasoning_efforts: "low,medium,high"`), persisting in `/app/data/storage.sqlite` across official Docker image upgrades without editing `MODEL_SPECS` in source code.
  - Create four top-level OmniRoute combos (`gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-medium`, `gemini-4-carbon-low`) pinned exclusively to the 3 Google AI Ultra `agy` accounts (`0532bad4-2486-4286-8f06-510e9c678620`, `338764b8-1cd7-4946-b789-ee5dad4c5073`, `62af823b-f3d7-4e6b-87a9-98069167d4f6`) with `strategy: "quota-weighted"`, `context_length: 1000000`, `context_cache_protection: false`, `config.targetTimeoutMs: 180000`, `config.predictiveTtftMs: 0`, `config.quotaWeightedFloorPercent: 5`, and 5-minute quota cache TTLs.
- **Eliminate Thundering-Herd Dispatch, Pro 429 Leaks, and Ultra Quota Bleed Across Existing Antigravity Combos**:
  - Transition multi-account `agy` combos (`gemini-4-argon{,-high,-medium,-low}`, `gemini-3.8-flash-high`, `claude-opus-5.5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`) to `strategy: "quota-weighted"` (`quotaWeightedFloorPercent: 5` on Ultra Carbon/Argon combos to protect low-quota accounts such as `62af823b`, `quotaWeightedFloorPercent: 1` on Pro Flash/Claude combos, `quotaWeightedScoring: "leftover"`, `resetAwareQuotaCacheTtlMs: 300000`, `resetWindowQuotaCacheTtlMs: 300000`), which atomically increments per-connection `inflight` in the same synchronous JS turn (`w = score / (1 + inflight)`) and filters out exhausted/floor accounts before dispatch.
  - Prune the 7 unentitled/exhausted Google AI Pro accounts from `gemini-4-argon`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `gemini-4-argon-medium`, and `gemini-4-argon-low` so they route strictly across the 3 entitled Ultra accounts (`0532bad4`, `338764b8`, `62af823b`).
  - Isolate `gemini-3.8-flash-high` to the 11 active Google AI Pro `agy` accounts (`79.6%–99.0%` remaining Flash quota) with `config.targetTimeoutMs: 120000` (raised from `25,000 ms` to prevent premature aborts on large-context `effort=high` turns), removing the 3 Ultra accounts (`62af823b`, `0532bad4`, `338764b8`) because Flash shares the 1,000-unit weekly Argon/Carbon bucket on Ultra accounts.
  - Expand `claude-opus-5.5` and `claude-sonnet-5.5` from 6 steps to all 11 active Google AI Pro `agy` accounts using each account's exact `syncedAvailableModels` entitlement (9 Pro accounts on native `agy/claude-opus-5-5-high` and `agy/claude-sonnet-5-5-high` at 100% quota + 2 Pro accounts `9d66311f` and `ce95e1cb` on `agy/claude-opus-4-6-thinking` and `agy/claude-sonnet-4-6`), excluding the 3 Ultra accounts (`0532bad4`, `338764b8`, `62af823b`) so Claude usage never triggers `genericQuotaFetcher.ts`'s unscoped `argon` family quota aggregation.
  - Normalize `rate_limit_protection = 0` (`rateLimitProtection: false`) across all 14 active `agy` connections (clearing the `1` flag currently set on `38782750`, `9d66311f`, and `ce95e1cb`) and raise `settings.requestQueue` to `{ concurrentRequests: 64, requestsPerMinute: 600, maxWaitMs: 15000 }`.
- **Register `gemini-4-carbon{,-high,-medium,-low}` Across Workstation Coding Agents**:
  - Expose `gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-medium`, and `gemini-4-carbon-low` under existing active OmniRoute providers in `omp` (`~/.omp/agent/models.yml` and `fallbackChains` in `~/.omp/agent/config.yml`), `pi`, `prime-agent`, `hermes`, `opencode`, `kilo`, `codex` (`~/.codex/cockpit-model-catalog.json`), `kimi` (`~/.kimi-code/config.toml`), `grok`, `goose`, `droid`, `claude` (`~/.claude/profiles/omniroute-pm.json`), `muse` (`~/.config/muse/settings.json`), `qwen`, and `zed` while keeping every tool's startup default model unchanged.

### Explicit Non-Goals
- Modifying any git-tracked TypeScript/JavaScript source files in `/Users/androidteam/Omniroute` (`src/**`, `open-sse/**`, `docker-compose.yml`) or adding new server-chunk regex patches to `container-entrypoint-compat.sh` (existing `gemini-(?:[34]...)` and `antigravityQuotaFamily.ts` logic already support `gemini-4-carbon-*` natively).
- Enabling `ANTIGRAVITY_ACCOUNT_LEASE_ENABLED` (must remain `"false"` in `feature_flags` so concurrent subagents can multiplex across the 3 Ultra accounts without `HTTP 503 POOL_BUSY`) or enabling `COMBO_AUTO_PRUNE_STALE_STEPS` (must remain `"false"`).
- Enabling Qdrant (`--profile memory`) or Bifrost (`--profile bifrost`) sidecar containers, or switching `/app/data` away from the `omniroute-data` named Docker volume.
- Changing any coding agent's default startup model (`omp` stays on `omniroute/gemini-4-argon`, `claude` `omniroute-pm.json` stays on `gemini-3.8-flash-high`, `muse` stays on `muse-spark-1.3`).

### Affected Ownership Boundaries
- **Central Planning Store (`~/Developer/platform/openspec-store`)**: Owns the OpenSpec change artifacts (`proposal.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`, and delta specs under `specs/`).
- **External Managed Gateway (`~/Omniroute` + `omniroute-data` SQLite volume)**: Owns untracked deployment configuration (`~/Omniroute/.env`, `~/Omniroute/docker-compose.override.yml`) and persisted SQLite state (`combos`, `model_capability_overrides`, `model_context_overrides`, `provider_connections`, `key_value` settings) mutated via authenticated management APIs (`http://127.0.0.1:20128/api/*`).
- **Workstation Operator Configurations (`/Users/androidteam/.*`)**: Owns local CLI/editor catalog files (`~/.omp/agent/{models.yml,config.yml}`, `~/.pi/agent/models.json`, `~/.prime/agent/models.json`, `~/.hermes/config.yaml`, `~/.config/opencode/opencode.json`, `~/.config/kilo/kilo.jsonc`, `~/.codex/cockpit-model-catalog.json`, `~/.kimi-code/config.toml`, `~/.grok/config.toml`, `~/.config/goose/custom_providers/custom_omniroute.json`, `~/.factory/settings.json`, `~/.claude/profiles/omniroute-pm.json`, `~/.config/muse/settings.json`, `~/.qwen/settings.json`, `~/.config/zed/settings.json`) backed up with mode `0600` and SHA-256 manifests under `~/.omniroute/backups/`.

## Impact

- **OmniRoute Gateway (`127.0.0.1:20128` / `127.0.0.1:20129`)**: Immediate non-queued heavy admission doubles from 32 (`16 + 16`) to 64 (`32 + 32`) concurrent agent requests; parked queue capacity increases 16× (`4 MB -> 64 MB`); active byte budget increases to `256 MB`; libuv threadpool scales `4 -> 32`; zero source-code drift ensures `~/Omniroute/scripts/update.sh` upgrades cleanly on every upstream release.
- **Antigravity (`agy`) Quota & Error Rate**: Eliminates `429 Resource has been exhausted` errors caused by Pro accounts in `gemini-4-argon*` and stops `gemini-3.8-flash-high` subagents from draining Ultra Argon/Carbon quota; distributes concurrent bursts across healthy accounts via synchronous `inflight`-weighted selection (`quota-weighted`).
- **Workstation Coding Agents (`omp`, `claude`, `codex`, `pi`, `prime-agent`, `hermes`, `opencode`, `kilo`, `kimi`, `grok`, `goose`, `droid`, `muse`, `qwen`, `zed`)**: Gains immediate access to `gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-medium`, and `gemini-4-carbon-low` (`1M` context, up to `262,144` output tokens) alongside existing Argon, Flash, Claude 5.5, DeepSeek, Kimi, and Muse combos.
