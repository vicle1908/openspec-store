# Proposal

## Why

The shared OmniRoute (`v3.8.53`) gateway on `iosteam-mac-mini` (`127.0.0.1:20128` / `127.0.0.1:20129`) does not yet expose Google's `gemini-4-hydrogen{,-xhigh,-high,-medium,-low}` (`1,048,576` context window, `262,144` max output tokens) combos or effort-routing rules, leaving `iosteam-mac-mini`'s OMP `task` subagent role on `gemini-3.8-flash-high`, leaving all 15 local coding-agent catalogs without Hydrogen selectors, and risking `HTTP 400 ("Combo name already exists")` or `compression_combo_assignments` (`default-caveman`) drift against the concurrent `add-omniroute-gemini-4-hydrogen-combo-and-config` plan on peer workstation `macbook-pro-m5` (`100.106.143.78`). Cooperating across both workstations with an idempotent gateway specification unlocks the 7 Hydrogen-entitled Antigravity (`agy`) accounts (`6` WTU Pro accounts with independent `Gemini` quota plus `1` active Ultra account `62af823b`) with zero git-tracked OmniRoute source modifications.
