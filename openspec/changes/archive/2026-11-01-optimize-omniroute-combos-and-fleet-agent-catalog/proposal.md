# Proposal

## Why

Although the local OmniRoute v3.8.52 gateway (`http://127.0.0.1:20128` / `http://127.0.0.1:20129`) now serves ten active multi-account and specialized routing combos (`gemini-3.8-flash-high`, `gemini-4-argon`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `gemini-4-argon-medium`, `gemini-4-argon-low`, `claude-opus-5.5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`, `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, and `muse-spark-1.3`), the workstation's 16 coding agents, CLIs, and editor integrations lack uniform access to the newest high-usage combos (`claude-opus-5.5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`, `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, and `muse-spark-1.3`), suffer from missing `x-omniroute-no-memory: "true"` prompt-cache protection headers, retain a newly banned Google Antigravity account (`ascenddevtwo@gmail.com` / `d5c3465f-9797-4b4b-a937-c3e653e168fb`) across three primary combos and OMP's credential store, and strip structured non-shell tool outputs (`eval`, `task`, `todo`, `mcp__*`) through `/v1/responses` RTK log filters.

Specifically:
1. **Banned Antigravity Account (`d5c3465f` / `ascenddevtwo@gmail.com`) & Incomplete Combo Metadata in OmniRoute (`storage.sqlite`)**:
   - Connection `d5c3465f-9797-4b4b-a937-c3e653e168fb` (`ascenddevtwo@gmail.com`) is now permanently disabled upstream (`is_active = 0`, `test_status = 'banned'`, `HTTP 403: This service has been disabled in this account`), leaving `14` active `agy` accounts (`11` `g1-pro-tier` and `3` `g1-ultra-tier`). However, `d5c3465f` remains hardcoded as step `[6]` in `gemini-3.8-flash-high` (`12 -> 11` steps), `claude-opus-5.5` (`15 -> 14` steps), and `claude-sonnet-5.5` (`15 -> 14` steps), and remains as row `id=11` (`email:ascenddevtwo@gmail.com`) in `~/.omp/agent/agent.db:auth_credentials`.
   - Combo `claude-sonnet-5.5` (`9447399c-856b-4f52-8820-b3bf3dc3f132`) has `context_length: null` instead of `1000000`, and `muse-spark-1.3` (`453c5dd3-f7f4-4c44-bd4b-6c3c1d0f4e5b`) step `[1]` (`agy/gemini-3.8-flash-medium`) lacks an explicit `connectionId` and email `label`.
2. **Incomplete High-Usage Combo Catalog Across the 16 Coding Agents & Editors**:
   - Multi-account Claude combos (`claude-opus-5.5` and `claude-sonnet-5.5`) and fast coding combos (`deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `muse-spark-1.3`) are missing from almost every coding CLI (`omp` lacks `claude-opus-5.5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`, and combo `muse-spark-1.3` while retaining single-connection `agy/claude-opus-4-6-thinking` and broken OAuth `mc/muse-spark-1.3`; `pi`, `prime-agent`, `hermes`, `opencode`, `kilo`, `codex`, `kimi-code`, `grok`, `goose`, `droid`, `claude`, `muse`, `qwen`, and `cline` lack `claude-opus-5.5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`, `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, and `kimi-k3.1-pro`; `zed` lacks `claude-sonnet-5.5`, `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, and combo `muse-spark-1.3`).
   - `kilo` (`~/.config/kilo/kilo.jsonc`) is missing `gemini-3.8-flash-high` under `provider.omniroute-responses.models` and still sets top-level `"model": "omniroute/sh/codex"` against a deactivated `sh` provider (`is_active = 0`).
   - `codex` (`~/.codex/cockpit-model-catalog.json`) omits its own default model `gemini-3.8-flash-high` from `models[]`, preventing interactive `/model` users from switching back to `gemini-3.8-flash-high` after selecting another slug.
   - `cline` (`~/.cline/data/settings/providers.json`) still targets `model: "pm/Claude-Fable"` (`anthropic-compatible-6408e370`, which fails 45/45 health checks with `HTTP 401`) with `reasoning.enabled: false`, and `zed` (`~/.config/zed/settings.json`) retains 25+ dead `dlg/*`, `dlg1/*`, `ollamacloud/*`, `cx/*`, and `gc/*` entries while defaulting to single-provider `agy/gemini-3.8-flash-high` instead of the combo `gemini-3.8-flash-high`.
3. **Missing `x-omniroute-no-memory: "true"` Header & `/v1/responses` RTK Non-Shell Tool Filtering**:
   - While `omp` and `pi` pass `"x-omniroute-no-memory": "true"` on their OmniRoute provider blocks to suppress gateway `Memory context:` prompt-prefix injection (preserving upstream prefix caches and delegating memory to dedicated `AgentMemory`), `prime-agent`, `hermes`, `opencode`, `kilo`, `codex`, `kimi-code`, and `goose` omit that header.
   - In `/Users/androidteam/Omniroute/scripts/container-entrypoint-compat.sh` (`R44`), `adaptBodyForCompression()` (`14211.js`, `41669.js`, `72459.js`, `88137.js`, `98542.js`) deletes `t.input` before `rtk` runs and sets `c._rtkSkip = !a.eligible` only for tools in `codexResponsesConfig.preserveToolNames`. Consequently, structured non-shell tools such as `eval`, `task`, `todo`, `find`, `ask`, and `mcp__*` over `/v1/responses` get `c._rtkSkip = false` and have ~80% of their non-error output lines stripped by `rtk` shell log filters.
