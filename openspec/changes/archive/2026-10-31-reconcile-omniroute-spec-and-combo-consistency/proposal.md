# Proposal

## Why

Post-archive verification of the October 2026 OmniRoute OpenSpec changes (`2026-10-31-document-omniroute-runtime-customization-matrix` and `2026-10-31-reconcile-omniroute-post-matrix-remediations`) against the live OmniRoute v3.8.52 deployment (`/Users/androidteam/Omniroute`) surfaced two classes of drift that break strict OpenSpec governance and live routing hygiene:
1. **Spec validation and cross-spec consistency failures**: `openspec validate --type spec --strict --store openspec-store` currently fails (`exit 1`) on `omniroute-runtime-customization` (2 requirements >500 chars synced during `5af5c1dd`), `omniroute-closure-integrity` (1 requirement >500 chars), and `omniroute-agent-cli-routing` (8 requirements >500 chars). In addition, `omniroute-runtime-customization` and `wiki/entities/omniroute.md` undercount the live `/Users/androidteam/Omniroute/scripts/container-entrypoint-compat.sh` inventory (`3` non-chunk patches + `43` chunk/route replacements = `46` modifications guarded by `3` non-chunk + `46` chunk markers = `49` fail-closed assertions across `19` compiled files, vs `41` replacements / `46` markers claimed), while `omniroute-compression-and-routing` and `omniroute-agent-cli-routing` retain stale internal scenario assertions (`25 enabled flags` including `COMBO_AUTO_PRUNE_STALE_STEPS`, `targetTimeoutMs: 25000` on `gemini-4-argon*` and `claude-opus-5.5`, `8 agy Opus accounts`, a stale `combo-ref: gemini-3.8-flash-high` claim on `claude-opus-5.5`, a stale byte-identical `/app/.build/next/server/chunks/*.js` claim on the Muse Code requirement, and an immediate probe-reservation delete claim on failed TTFT probes).
2. **Live SQLite `omniroute-data` combo and `agy` account pool drift**: After connection `ce95e1cb-27a4-433f-9c50-bdb6ad0b6b6b` (`vicgnome19097@gmail.com`) was upgraded to an active `g1-pro-tier` account, OmniRoute now has **15 active `agy` accounts** (`12` `g1-pro-tier` + `3` `g1-ultra-tier`, `0` `free-tier`, and `1` deactivated/banned account `47f334b1-b801-41f6-a709-4ebabb7a86d6`). However, `gemini-3.8-flash-high` (`7555d851-b4f3-44c6-b1fb-c6fdceabf388`) only pools 11 Pro accounts (omitting `ce95e1cb-27a4-433f-9c50-bdb6ad0b6b6b`) and omits explicit `"context_cache_protection": false` in `combos.data`; meanwhile, `claude-opus-5.5` (`4b2430da-0b3b-4b0e-9773-ec0f97adc252`) and `claude-sonnet-5.5` (`9447399c-856b-4f52-8820-b3bf3dc3f132`) still include banned connection `47f334b1-b801-41f6-a709-4ebabb7a86d6` (`vicgnome19091@gmail.com`) at step `[4]` while omitting active Pro connection `e85dd569-0271-4974-bcd9-b250295e6810` (`hongsonle19081952@gmail.com`).
