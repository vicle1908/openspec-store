# Proposal: Remediate NPM/PNPM Audit Regressions and Enforce Home-Directory Prefix Hygiene

## Why

Stray package-manager artifacts recreated in `/Users/androidteam` (`~/node_modules`, `~/pnpm-lock.yaml`, `~/pnpm-workspace.yaml`, `~/bun.lock`) hijack `npm prefix` resolution from `/Users/androidteam/Developer` and cause `npm audit` / `npm audit fix` to crash with `ENOLOCK`, while newly published upstream advisories (`handlebars`, `http-cache-semantics`, `source-map-js`, `proxy-addr`, `shell-quote`, `compression`, `postcss-selector-parser`, `electron`, `smol-toml`, `katex`) and stale override pins have caused audit and specification drift across `~/.local/share/home-toolchain`, `platform/mcp-router`, `tdt/realtime/frontend`, `legacy/kafka-microservices`, and `openspec/specs/npm-audit-remediation/spec.md`.

Specifically:
1. **Home-Directory Prefix Re-Hijacking (`ENOLOCK`)**: Even after `/Users/androidteam/package.json` was removed by `2026-10-04-relocate-home-manifest-to-stop-prefix-hijack`, `/Users/androidteam/node_modules` (alongside `~/pnpm-lock.yaml`, `~/pnpm-workspace.yaml`, `~/bun.lock`, `~/bun.lock.bun-backup-20260813_100505`, and `~/package.json.bun-backup-20260813_100505`) exists directly in `$HOME`. Because `@npmcli/config` (`Config.loadLocalPrefix()`) walks upward matching `hasPackageJson || dirExists(p, 'node_modules')`, running `npm prefix`, `npm audit`, or `npm audit fix` from `/Users/androidteam/Developer` resolves to `/Users/androidteam` and fails with `ENOLOCK: loadVirtual requires existing shrinkwrap file`. Meanwhile, `scripts/inventory-workstation-toolchain.py` only inspects `npm config get prefix` (`~/.npm-global`) and `~/.local/lib/node_modules` (`adhoc-local`), failing to detect `$HOME` `node_modules` or stray lockfiles.
2. **Global NPM Update Audit Prompt (`EAUDITGLOBAL`) & `~/.npm-global` Hygiene**: During `npm update -g`, `Arborist.reify()` submits a quick audit of `/Users/androidteam/.npm-global/lib/node_modules` and prints `run npm audit fix`, even though `npm audit -g` fails by design with `EAUDITGLOBAL`. Out of 16 global packages in `~/.npm-global`, 14 have 0 vulnerabilities and 2 (`@agentmemory/agentmemory@0.9.30` and `happy@1.2.5`, both already at their latest published npm versions) carry transitive upstream advisories. Additionally, `~/.npm-global/bin` retains 3 stale dangling `.bin-HASH` retired symlinks (`.amp-1TDgNqTm`, `.dgrep-EmxC0FXp`, `.tsc-w3Kv8tVZ`) and `~/.npm-global/lib/node_modules` retains 8 empty scope directories from retired installs.
3. **Relocated Workstation Toolchain (`~/.local/share/home-toolchain`) & Spec Drift**:
   - `~/.local/share/home-toolchain/package.json` declares 4 direct `dependencies` (`@usebruno/cli`, `newman-reporter-htmlextra`, `pyright`, `yaml-language-server`), 22 top-level string `overrides`, and 1 scoped object override (`@usebruno/requests`), with `node_modules/newman` (`6.2.2`) resolved automatically via `newman-reporter-htmlextra@1.23.1`'s `peerDependencies: { "newman": "^6.0.0" }`.
   - `overrides.handlebars` is pinned at `"4.7.9"`, which now falls inside 3 advisories (`GHSA-8r5x-fm3f-whwj` Critical, `GHSA-p8wg-vrv2-v86f` Critical, `GHSA-xw65-4hp5-5hc7` Moderate; patched in `4.7.10`), inflating `npm audit` to 11 vulnerabilities (`4 critical, 7 high`) instead of the 10 high (`0 critical`) irreducible residuals.
   - Direct dependencies `@usebruno/cli` (`4.2.0` vs latest `4.2.1`) and `yaml-language-server` (`1.24.0` vs latest `2.0.0`), peer `newman` (`6.2.2` vs latest `6.2.3`), and override pins (`uuid` `14.0.2 -> 14.0.3`, `qs` `6.16.0 -> 6.16.1`, `fast-uri` `4.2.1 -> 4.2.2`, `ip-address` `10.7.3 -> 10.7.4`, `hono` `4.13.12 -> 4.13.13`, plus `js-yaml` `5.4.2 -> 5.5.0` via `>=4.3.2`) lag their latest npm releases.
   - `npm-audit-remediation/spec.md` still states `12 high` in multiple scenarios (contradicting the `10 high` outcome after the scoped `@usebruno/requests` `@faker-js/faker` override), still references `~` on line 107, and still lists `platform/prime-agent` as an active `main`-branch Node.js manifest surface even though `platform/prime-agent` on `main` is now a Rust Cargo workspace (`Cargo.toml` / `deny.toml`, with TypeScript manifests preserved on `backup/ts-custom-patches`).
4. **Governed Repository Audit Drift (`platform/mcp-router`, `tdt/realtime/frontend`, `legacy/kafka-microservices`)**:
   - `platform/mcp-router`: `pnpm audit` reports 15 advisories (`2 critical, 11 high, 2 moderate`), of which 10 have published patches (`proxy-addr >=2.0.8`, `shell-quote >=1.11.0`, `compression >=1.8.2`, `http-cache-semantics >=4.3.0`, `source-map-js >=1.2.2`, `postcss-selector-parser >=7.1.6`, `electron >=41.10.7`) and 5 are unpatched upstream residuals (`extract-zip <=2.0.1` x2, `braces <=3.0.3`, `node-forge <=1.4.0`, `sprintf-js <=1.1.3`). Additionally, `pnpm@12.14.0` requires `blockExoticSubdeps: false` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` to resolve `@electron/rebuild`'s `@electron/node-gyp` git dependency when updating the lockfile.
   - `tdt/realtime/frontend`: `npm audit` reports 2 High advisories (`http-cache-semantics <=4.2.0` and `source-map-js <1.2.2`) that now have published upstream fixes (`4.3.0` and `1.2.2`), plus a stale gitignored Finder duplicate `package-lock 2.json` (matched by `tdt/realtime/.gitignore:64`).
   - `legacy/kafka-microservices`: `npm audit` reports 9 advisories (`5 high, 1 moderate, 3 low`), of which 4 (`smol-toml <1.9.1` Moderate and `katex <0.19.0` chain Low x3) are resolved via `"overrides": { "smol-toml": ">=1.9.1", "katex": ">=0.19.0" }`, leaving only the 5 High advisories rooted in unpatched `braces <=3.0.3`.
